Request Account Deletion

Masuka Junior School Learning App

You may request the deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time. Because accounts in this app are managed by the school, deletion requests must be submitted to the school administrator.

How to request deletion:

Send an email to: suarezmudimu@gmail.com

Please include:
  • Your full name (or the student's name if requesting on behalf of a child)
  • Your role: Student / Teacher / Administrator
  • The request: "Please delete my account and all associated data"
We will process your request within 30 days.

What data will be deleted

  • Your account login credentials
  • Your name and profile information
  • All lesson progress and assessment records linked to your account
  • Any media files (drawings, photos, videos) you have uploaded

Data that may be retained

Anonymised or aggregated records (e.g. overall class statistics with no personal identifiers) may be retained for school reporting purposes. No personally identifiable information is kept after a deletion request is fulfilled.

Questions?

If you have any questions, contact us at suarezmudimu@gmail.com.
